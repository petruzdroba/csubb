Welcome to the National Park Service

Explore Romania's most beautiful national parks and nature reserves. Report trail hazards, browse hiking routes, or join our ranger team.



Our most beautiful parks!

Retezat National Park

Romania's oldest national park, founded in 1935. Home to over 80 glacial lakes, virgin forests, and the highest peak Peleaga at 2,505m. A true mountaineer's paradise in the Southern Carpathians.

Călimani National Park

Built on volcanic origins, Călimani features the largest inactive caldera in Europe — 10km wide and 800m deep. Famous for the eerie "12 Apostles" rock formations and the peak Pietrosul at 2,103m.

Cheile Bicazului-Hășmaș

A dramatic canyon carved by the Bicaz River with rock walls rising up to 400m. Home to the haunting Red Lake, formed by a natural landslide in 1837, with submerged tree trunks still visible above water.

Nera-Beușnița National Park

A remote canyon park in the Anina Mountains near the Serbian border. Famous for turquoise pools, the Beușnița Waterfall, and Ochiul Beiului Lake. Expect wild gorges, lynxes, and very few tourists.

Buila-Vânturarița National Park

A compact limestone massif in the Southern Carpathians, known for its vertical cliffs, ancient monasteries, and rich biodiversity. One of Romania's smallest national parks but among the most scenic.
