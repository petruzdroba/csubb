From fairest creatures we desire increase,therefore let time decide what beauty means in thee.
The wind moves slowly through the summer trees,and rivers hold the light of fading day.
I left behind a world I could not save,and walked alone into the quiet night.
The sky is cold and deep and wide,no place for truth or place to hide.
We are made of fragments and forgotten light,trying to become something whole again.
Am mers prin ploaie fără să privesc înapoi,și am învățat că tăcerea spune mai mult decât noi.
